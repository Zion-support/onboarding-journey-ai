## What it does

Onboarding Journey AI generates a personalized onboarding plan for every new hire — role-specific milestones, intro meetings, and training — then nudges managers and buddies so nobody falls through the cracks.

## Key features

- **Role-based 30/60/90 plans** — generated from the job description and team context.
- **Smart nudges** — managers, buddies, and IT reminded at the right moments.
- **Progress dashboards** — onboarding health per hire, cohort, and department.
- **Feedback loops** — new-hire check-ins analyzed for friction points.
- **Tool provisioning checklist** — accounts and access tracked to done.

## Who it's for

HR and team leads at growing companies where onboarding quality varies by manager.
